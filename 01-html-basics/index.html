About TechNova

Technova is the annual technical fest of ABC college. It brings young minds to learn , compete and showcase their talent. The event will be held on 20th & 21st March 2026. Students from all department can participate.

List of Events

  • Coding Competition
  • web design challenge
  • Techincal Quiz
  • Poster Making
  • Debugging challenge

Important Information

  1. Registration Deadline : 15th March 2026
  2. Eligibility : Open for all students.
  3. Participation : Individual or Team (max 2 Members)
  4. Certificate will be provided to all participants.
  5. Winners will be awarded exciting prizes.
